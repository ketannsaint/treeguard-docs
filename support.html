Support

Practical help for configuration, execution, and data requests.

Contact Email support@apps.themoonknows.com. Do not send passwords, API tokens, private keys, or unrelated Jira content.

What to include

Include the Jira Cloud hostname, project key, app environment, approximate time, execution status, and any issue keys shown in the receipt.

Set up one real process

During the 30-day Marketplace evaluation, share the shape of one release, onboarding, or incident checklist and we will help configure the first template at no cost. This is a guided setup, not custom development.

Response targets

Priority Example Initial response
Critical Security incident or widespread destructive behavior 1 business day
High App unavailable or residual Jira issues after failure 1 business day
Normal Configuration, isolated error, or data request 2 business days
Low Feature request or documentation feedback 5 business days

Targets are not uptime guarantees or guaranteed resolution times. Atlassian platform incidents may require coordination with Atlassian.

Security reports

Send suspected vulnerabilities privately to security@apps.themoonknows.com. Include reproducible steps and impact, but do not access data that does not belong to you.
